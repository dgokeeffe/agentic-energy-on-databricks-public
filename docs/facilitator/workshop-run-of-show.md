# Workshop run of show

This is the workshop's only clock source. The current participant path is the
shared GitHub-issue lifecycle in [`QUICKSTART.md`](../../QUICKSTART.md); retained
lane directories are historical material and are not routed.

## Roles

Each pair keeps one business outcome responsibility and one engineering quality
responsibility. A facilitator releases prepared fixtures and controls all
workspace operations. A person other than the author approves the plan and
performs or accepts independent review.

## Facilitator limits

Participants stay in local or read-only checks. They may inspect
`nemweb_foundation/` but do not deploy it. Only a facilitator with current
approval may deploy, run a job, provision Lakebase, change grants, enable live
data or a preview, or change a schedule. Every workspace-aware command includes
`--profile daveok`. Prepared evidence remains labelled non-live.

Switch to the [fallback](workshop-fallback.md) when a named capability is not
released by its checkpoint. An unavailable platform step is `not-run` or
`blocked`; it never becomes a pass from fixture output.
