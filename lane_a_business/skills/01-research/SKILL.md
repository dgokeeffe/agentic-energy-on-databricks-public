# NEMWEB research and brief

Use this skill for Track A cards 1–5. Read the
[playbook](../../../docs/participant/workshop-playbook.md#section-one-specify-and-deliver-the-work),
[pair record](../../../docs/participant/workshop-pair-record.md), and
[fallback pack](../../../docs/facilitator/workshop-fallback.md).

## Research record

Record:

- decision owner and the operational decision the trend informs;
- source title or URL, publisher, capture time, and evidence status;
- five-minute interval-ending grain and region;
- fixed AEST market time and UTC processing timestamps;
- regional price in AUD/MWh and demand in MW;
- correction ordering, intervention treatment, and effective-run rule;
- newest source publication and newest Gold publication;
- reasons the trend could be wrong, including stale data, a late correction,
  timezone error, intervention double counting, or missing context; and
- what the paired Track B owner must prove about processing time.

Use the default question unless the facilitator approves another. A human who
did not draft the brief approves measurable acceptance criteria before the
first run.

## Gold-window evidence

Save the query or method, selected 24-hour window, row count, newest source time,
newest Gold time, observed trend, uncertainty, and human decision. Use
`is_effective_run = true`. Mark the result live, snapshot, or prepared fallback.
A prepared extract supports the exercise but does not prove current freshness.

Stop if the source contract cannot be cited, the question needs another report
family, the approver is also the author, the window mixes intervention runs, or
a prepared result is about to be called live.
