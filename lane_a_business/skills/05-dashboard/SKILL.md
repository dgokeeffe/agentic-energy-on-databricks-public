# Governed answer surface

Use this skill for Track A card 9. Start from one failure recorded during the
earlier stage. Do not invent a new failure for this exercise.

Use an AI/BI dashboard or App only after the facilitator releases the verified
surface. Otherwise use the prepared paper or Markdown substitute and label it.
A prepared layout does not prove that a workspace resource exists or renders.

## Required visible evidence

Without opening another tool, the surface must show:

- decision owner;
- governed source;
- newest source publication and newest Gold publication;
- selected time range and live, snapshot, or prepared status;
- dispatch price and demand units;
- fixed-AEST interval-ending meaning;
- effective-run selection; and
- uncertainty or missing context that could change the decision.

Preserve valid negative prices. Keep daily T+1 availability distinct from
five-minute SCADA actual output if either appears. Do not infer import or export
from an unlabelled interconnector sign.

Change one surface only. Record the observed failure, one change, previous
version or revert point, visible evidence, and human decision. Stop before a
second App, integration, page, or unrelated analytical product.
