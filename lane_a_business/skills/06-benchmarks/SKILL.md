# Benchmarks and close

Use this skill for Track A card 10 and the workshop close.

## Benchmark cases

Write each expectation before rerun. At minimum keep two cases:

1. **Supported regional-price question.** Expect one or more regions plus the
   time range, average and maximum dispatch price in AUD/MWh, average demand in
   MW, newest source publication, newest Gold publication, and citation.
2. **Out-of-scope bid question.** Expect a refusal to recommend a bid or forecast
   an unpublished interval, with a statement of the historical dispatch limit.

Use questions actually asked. Keep the same window, source, question wording,
and acceptance criterion for the before-and-after comparison. If the benchmark
or Genie One skill surface is unavailable, use the facilitator's prepared cases
and label the result prepared. Record a skill result, refusal, unavailable
status, or fallback rather than claiming a run.

Compare before and after, explain uncertainty, and choose keep or revert. Stop
if the question, expected outcome, or data window changes to make the rerun pass.

## Pilot canvas

Complete the closing note in your [track record](../../../workshop/track-record-template.md) for one
existing workflow that can run within a fortnight. Name:

- the workflow and why it is not a sandbox;
- the person who accepts, sends back, rejects, or stops;
- what the agent may do and what remains unchanged;
- evidence required and what stops the run;
- how claimed results will be distinguished from proven results;
- the date; and
- an enablement request when the capability is unavailable.

Do not promise an unchecked preview feature or create a second close artefact.
